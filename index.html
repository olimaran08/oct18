10 / Good to know

Questions before we begin.

If your question is not here, tell us about your project and we'll help you understand the next step.

How accurate is the online estimate?

It is an early planning guide, not a quotation. The final cost depends on measured quantities, layout, material and hardware choices, site conditions and the exact scope. We confirm the price in a written BOQ or proposal.

Can I plan only my modular kitchen?

Yes. Select “Kitchen only” in the cost planner, then adjust your home size, finish level and optional work. A measured kitchen layout is needed for a formal quote.

What is the difference between essential, premium and luxury?

These are broad finish directions, not universal material guarantees. The actual specification — board type, shutter finish, hardware, countertop and accessories — should be listed clearly in your proposal.

Does the estimate include GST and appliances?

The displayed estimate excludes GST, appliances, loose furniture and major structural or civil work unless specifically included. Confirm tax treatment and inclusions in the final written quotation.

Do you work only in Chennai?

RAY is based in Chennai. Project feasibility outside our usual service area depends on scope, location, timeline and site coordination needs; contact us to discuss your location.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We review the project brief, discuss the site and requirements, and agree on the next step. You can share a floor plan or reference images when you contact us.
